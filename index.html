👥 Teams & Scoreboard

1–5 teams share the scoreboard across every game. Your room has 3 desk columns, so 3 is the default. Tap a team on the scoreboard anytime to fix its score by any place value.

🎯 Quiz — add questions

One per line, * marks the correct answer. On-screen answer order is shuffled automatically.
MC | Who led Israel out of Egypt? | Moses* | David | Noah | Paul
TF | Noah built an ark. | True
FB | Jesus turned water into ___. | wine

🕵️ Who Am I? — add clue sets

One set per line: the answer first, then clues from hardest to easiest (early guesses are worth more).
Moses | Born a slave, raised a prince | Met God in a burning bush | Sent the ten plagues | Parted the Red Sea

🏆 Jeopardy — build a board

One clue per line. Start each line with the round: S = Jeopardy, D = Double Jeopardy, F = Final. A random Daily Double is hidden each game (2 in the Double round).
S | Old Testament | 100 | Who built the ark? | Noah
D | Kings | 200 | Who was the first king of Israel? | Saul
F | The Gospel | What must you do to be saved? | Believe in Jesus
Leaving off S/D/F is treated as a single Jeopardy round.

🧩 Concentration — match sets & pictures

Each set is a pile of matching pairs (12 are used per game on the 5×5 board). One item per line makes a pair of the same word; use A | B to match two different things.
Lamb of God  (matches another "Lamb of God" tile)
Bethlehem | Jesus was born here  (matches a place to its fact)
Pictures: put image files in an images folder in your repo, then just type the filename (e.g. shepherd.jpg) on the game screen. You can also set a default image below.

⏱️ Beat the Clock — categories

One category prompt per line — the kind of thing a team can rattle off. The teacher taps +1 for each right answer against the clock.
Name the Twelve Disciples
Name animals that were on Noah's ark

🎭 Reverse Charades — word sets

Each set is a title plus words/phrases to act out — one per line. The word shows big on screen; the acting team's guesser faces away.
Parting the Red Sea
Noah building the ark

🏆 Word Puzzle Wheel — puzzles

One puzzle per line: Category | PHRASE. Keep phrases fairly short so they fit the board. The wheel already has all the money spaces plus Bankrupt, Lose a Turn, and a $5,000 wedge.
Bible Verse | THE LORD IS MY SHEPHERD
Bible Person | JOHN THE BAPTIST
